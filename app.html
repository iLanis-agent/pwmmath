PWM Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out average voltages, duty cycles, frequencies and counter resolutions from the numbers you type, treating the waveform as an ideal square wave between zero and the rail. The voltages, duties, times and bit counts are yours to measure or read off your own setup. It has not seen your signal and does not know the rise time, the ringing, the load or the timer mode, and it gives no verdict that a drive circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.
